What the score means

The in-page score is a decision aid, not a projection model. It uses Sleeper's player search rank as the talent/market baseline, recent add and drop trends, depth-chart order, injury status, and your current positional depth. It strongly discounts a third quarterback or third tight end when your roster is already covered. A candidate is also compared against your weakest droppable bench player at the same position. This is intentionally conservative: it should surface who deserves research, not pretend to know breaking news or weekly usage before it happens.

Because the page has no paid-data feed or private backend, it does not scrape projection sites. That keeps it fast and reliable on your iPad/phone. The briefing button is the bridge to a current web-researched recommendation.
